Cruises from Mobile, AL

The port that doesn’t need an airport.

Alabama’s cruise port is a drive, not a flight. Carnival runs two ships from downtown Mobile — one this fall and winter, one year-round from next May.

About 2.5 hours from Clanton, straight down I-65.

The Alabama Cruise Terminal is downtown Mobile: park the car, walk on board. No TSA line, no checked bag, no flight — just the drive, and Carnival does the rest from there.
