Puerto Vallarta Cruise & Stay

See it from the water. Stay for the week.

A 7-night Mexican Riviera cruise from Los Angeles, then unpack for a week of all-inclusive at Hard Rock Hotel Vallarta. Two vacations, one trip.

The combo

Cruise the Riviera, then live on Banderas Bay.

The cruise takes you down the Mexican Riviera — Cabo San Lucas, Mazatlán, Puerto Vallarta — with ship life as the vacation and the ports as the appetizers. When it docks, the vacation keeps going: a week of all-inclusive at Hard Rock Hotel Vallarta in Nuevo Vallarta, a few minutes up the bay.

  • 7-night Mexican Riviera cruises from ~$529 pp (Norwegian, round-trip Los Angeles, taxes and port fees included)
  • Carnival sails from Long Beach; Royal Caribbean’s Navigator and Princess also run the route
  • All-inclusive nights at Hard Rock Hotel Vallarta from ~$310/night (sample)
  • Flights, transfers, cruise, and resort — we book the whole chain so nothing drops

Why it works

Two kinds of vacation in one trip.

The ship week

Wake up somewhere new every morning — Cabo’s arch, Mazatlán’s old town, PV’s Malecón. Pools, shows, and dinners on board, unpacked once.

The resort week

Then you stop moving: a balcony over Banderas Bay, six restaurants, a swim-up bar, kids’ pool, and the Rock Spa. The cruise gets you there rested; the resort sends you home rested.

Sample math: cruise from ~$529 pp + seven nights at the resort from ~$310/night. Real dates move everything — we confirm every number live at booking.
