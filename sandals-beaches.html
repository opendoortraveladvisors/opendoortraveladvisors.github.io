Sandals & Beaches

Two all-inclusive legends. One door.

Sandals for couples, Beaches for families. Everything included, one price, and booking through us costs you nothing extra — the resorts pay us.

Sandals · adults only

Sandals: made for two.

Seventeen adults-only resorts across Jamaica, The Bahamas, Grenada, Barbados, St. Lucia, Antigua, and Curaçao. Gourmet dining, premium drinks, transfers, tips — all included, all in the published price.

  • Entry rooms from about $206–263 pp/night (Sandals Negril sample)
  • A 7-night couples’ stay typically lands $4,000–$7,000 all-in, excluding flights (Sandals’ own guidance)
  • Select 7-night bookings pay for 6 — a free night on many room categories
  • Honeymoons, vow renewals, and anniversary extras handled by a Royal Weddings-certified planner

Beaches · family

Beaches: the whole crew, one price.

Beaches takes the Sandals standard and opens it to kids — waterparks, Sesame Street character experiences, and everything included. Three resorts: Turks & Caicos on Grace Bay, Negril on Seven Mile Beach, and Ocho Rios.

  • Adults from ~$329 pp/night, kids from ~$38 pp/night (Beaches Turks & Caicos sample; kids 2–15 sharing with two adults)
  • A family of four, 7 nights all-in with flights: roughly $5,700–$8,900 (Negril, luxury level)
  • Sesame Street character breakfasts and experiences included
  • Waterparks, kids’ camps, and babysitting so the adults get an evening too
