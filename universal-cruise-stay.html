Universal Cruise & Stay Vacay

A cruise, a resort, one package.

Three nights on the water, four nights on property at Universal Orlando, park days in between. One hour from ship to hotel, sold as one trip.

How it works

Ship first. Then the parks.

Friday to Monday, you’re on Utopia of the Seas out of Port Canaveral — Nassau and Perfect Day at CocoCay, zero sea days, so the vacation starts the moment you board. Monday morning you walk off the ship and an hour later you’re checking in on property at Universal Orlando, where you stay four nights with park-to-park tickets.

  • 3-night cruise from ~$600 pp, Fri–Mon, Nassau + Perfect Day at CocoCay
  • On-site stays from ~$96/night — 30% off stays of 5+ nights (Sep 8, 2026 – Mar 15, 2027)
  • Buy 3, Get 2 Free: 3 park days become 5, through Apr 24, 2027
  • Early Park Admission + free shuttles included on-site

Year-round, dressed for the season
